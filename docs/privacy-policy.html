Overview

Orbit ("we", "our", or "us") is a language learning app that helps you have real conversations in your target language. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

We collect only what's necessary to make the app work and improve your learning experience. We do not sell your data.

📋

Data We Collect

  • Account information — your email address, used to create and manage your account.
  • Language & learning preferences — the language you're learning, your proficiency level, your city, and interests you select during onboarding.
  • Audio recordings — voice recordings made during coaching sessions. These are processed to analyze pronunciation and grammar, then discarded. We do not store raw audio.
  • Conversation data — the text content of your sessions with Sol, used to personalize future conversations and track your progress.
  • Usage data — session counts, session duration, and feature usage, used to generate your weekly progress reports.
  • Grammar & pronunciation mistakes — specific errors flagged during coaching, stored to help Sol avoid repeating corrections you've already received.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active subscription, managed through Apple's in-app purchase system.
🚫

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise GPS location — only the city you manually selected.
  • We do not store raw audio files.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, or any data unrelated to language learning.
🔧

How We Use Your Data

  • To personalize your AI coach (Sol) based on your city, interests, and learning level.
  • To track your progress and generate weekly reports.
  • To improve Sol's conversation suggestions over time based on what you engage with.
  • To manage your account and subscription.
  • To detect and fix bugs and improve app performance.
🤝

Third-Party Services

Orbit uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini API — powers Sol's AI conversations and analysis. Conversation content is sent to Google's API to generate responses. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Supabase — our cloud database and authentication provider. Your account data and learning history are stored securely on Supabase-hosted servers. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • Apple — handles all in-app purchases and subscriptions via StoreKit. We do not see or store your payment information. Apple Privacy Policy →
💾

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in two places:

  • On your device — preferences, conversation pool, and session data are stored locally using iOS's secure storage (UserDefaults with App Group protection).
  • In the cloud — session history, mistake tracking, and progress reports are stored in our Supabase database, protected by row-level security so only you can access your own data.

We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

🧒

Children's Privacy

Orbit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.
