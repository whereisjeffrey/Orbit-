## Where to Find Partners

### Instagram / TikTok
- Hashtags: #expatlife, #digitalnomad, #vidaenmexico, #gringosinbrazil, #expatinmexico, #livingabroad, #nomadlife
- Target: 5K-50K followers (big enough to matter, small enough to reply)
- Look for accounts posting daily about life abroad

### YouTube
- Search: "moving to Mexico", "expat life Brazil", "digital nomad Colombia", "learning Spanish abroad"
- Target: 2K-50K subscribers
- Business email in video descriptions

### Facebook Groups
- "Expats in Mexico City" (200K+ members)
- "Digital Nomads Mexico"
- "Americans in Brazil"
- "Foreigners in Colombia"
- Don't pitch in group — find the admin, DM directly

### Blogs / Newsletters
- Google: "best apps for expats 2026", "expat tools Mexico", "digital nomad toolkit"
- Target roundup article writers — always looking for new products
- Use Hunter.io to find email from domain

### Podcasts
- Search Apple Podcasts: "expat", "digital nomad", "living abroad"
- Pitch as a guest: "I moved to Brazil, couldn't text anyone, so I built a keyboard that translates in local slang"

### Relocation Services / Visa Agencies
- Google: "Mexico relocation service", "Brazil visa agency expats"
- Offer Orbit in their welcome package for new clients

### Coworking Spaces
- Selina, WeWork, local spots in CDMX, Medellin, Lisbon, Bali
- Contact community manager — they send weekly newsletters
- Offer: their members get a free month

## Daily Outreach Routine

1. 30 min finding 10 people
2. Send 10 personalized emails (customize first line per person)
3. Follow up after 3 days on non-replies
4. Track in spreadsheet: Name, Platform, Followers, Date Sent, Status

10/day = 70/week = 280/month
5% response rate = 14 conversations/month
3 converted partners = 3 communities promoting Orbit
